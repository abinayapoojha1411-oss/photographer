The Journey

From an Old 35mm Rangefinder in Old Delhi to Palaces Across the Globe

My fascination with photography began when my grandfather gifted me an old mechanical 35mm rangefinder camera during a winter walk through the narrow, light-dappled alleys of Chandni Chowk in Delhi. There was no instant digital preview, no rapid bursts—only the deliberate discipline of observing the light, waiting for the split second where human emotion stripped away all pretense.

After spending four years apprenticing under leading editorial fashion directors in Mumbai, I discovered my true calling when photographing my sister’s wedding in Rajasthan. Watching the raw, sacred gravity of centuries-old rituals unfold alongside quiet, private laughter made me realize that weddings are the most complex, beautiful theater on earth.

“The Art of the Unstaged”

Anyone can photograph a well-lit pose, but it takes patience and deep empathy to photograph a feeling. I strive to blend seamlessly into your celebration like an invited guest with an artist’s eye, ensuring you and your families are immersed in the moment rather than performing for the lens.

Our Approach & Technical Discipline

State-of-the-Art Gear

Dual high-resolution Sony Alpha full-frame bodies paired with Sony G-Master prime lenses (35mm f/1.4, 50mm f/1.2, 85mm f/1.4) for unmatched low-light creamy bokeh.

Natural Light Mastery

We harness directional ambient sunlight, candle glow, and architectural chiaroscuro to produce rich, three-dimensional depth without harsh, jarring flash.

Bespoke Color Alchemy

Every delivered image is individually hand-retouched and color-graded using custom film emulation profiles inspired by Kodak Portra and Fuji 400H.

Archival Heirlooms

Printed albums bound in genuine Italian leather and archival cotton rag paper engineered to withstand generations without fading or yellowing.
